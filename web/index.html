01 / Open the fitted surface.

Five strikes at each of three expiries

The fit minimizes equally weighted squared call-price errors across 15 quotes: strikes 80, 90, 100, 110, 120 at 0.25, 1 and 2 years. This is not an implied-volatility or vega-weighted objective. A smaller error can favor higher-priced options.

Stochastic variance.

Under the stated risk-neutral model, dS/S = (r − q)dt + √v dW₁ and dv = κ(θ − v)dt + ξ√v dW₂, with correlated Brownian shocks ρ. v₀ and θ are annualized variances, not volatilities. Time is in years. ξ is volatility of variance, not spot volatility.

One diagnostic, not a verdict.

Feller positivity is not an optimizer constraint here. Failing the strict sufficient condition does not invalidate Fourier pricing; passing it does not establish a good economic model. No variance paths are simulated.

Three starts can agree in a narrow valley. Agreement is not proof of unique parameters, statistical identification, a global optimum or hedge quality. Synthetic truth is used to create the fixture and evaluate withheld prices, never supplied to the optimizer.

Integration has limits.

A decaying-exponential Heston characteristic function and composite Gauss-Legendre rule price each call. The refinement gap compares 16-node panels to 32-node panels with a longer cutoff. It is a diagnostic, not a proved integration error bound. Only the declared parameter, strike and expiry box is supported.

Noise is invented too.

Relative bounded-uniform price perturbations are not a market microstructure model. Noisy targets are not forced to preserve static arbitrage. The fit uses unrounded prices, no bid-ask intervals or confidence estimates. Same-model generation makes this easier than real calibration.

Keep the boundary.

No historical quotes, orders, Greeks, hedging PnL, transaction costs or production calibration claim. A finite search is best-found, not guaranteed optimal. All work stays local after the static files load. Native Safari/Android untested.
